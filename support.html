Depthray

The quickest way to report a problem is from inside Depthray: open the settings, go to About and choose Report a problem. It sends the app's log and the headset details to Woodford XR over an encrypted connection, with a short note from you about what happened and, if you want a reply, your email address.

Its privacy policy and terms of use.

For anything else, write to support@woodfordxr.com.
