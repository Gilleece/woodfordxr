Depthray

The quickest way to report a problem is from inside Depthray: open the settings, go to About and choose Report a problem. It gathers the app's log and the headset details and hands them to your mail app, with a short note from you about what happened.

Its privacy policy is here.

For anything else, write to support@woodfordxr.com.
