About

Woodford XR is an independent VR software studio in Belfast, Northern Ireland.

It makes Depthray, the video player that turns any 2D video into 3D in real time on Meta Quest and PICO headsets, and publishes free and open source VR ports and mods on GitHub.

Woodford XR is the trading name of Sean Gilleece. Write to support@woodfordxr.com.
