Depthray

Available now

  • Meta Quest↗
  • PICO↗

Instant 2D to 3D from any video source. Depthray plays the films you already own and makes them 3D while they play, on the headset itself. No conversion step, no PC, no waiting.

Sources
Headset storage, USB drives, SMB shares, DLNA servers, Plex, Emby and Jellyfin, with Plex and Emby account sign-in. YouTube too, live streams included.
Formats
H.264, HEVC, VP9 and AV1 up to 4K at 120 fps, 8 and 10 bit, in mp4, mkv, mov, webm, ts, m2ts and more. HDR10, HDR10+, HLG and Dolby Vision.
3D
Side by side, over under, 180 and 360 detected from the file, with a manual override remembered per file. Brightness, contrast, gamma and saturation controls.
Sound
Virtual surround with 5.1 and 7.1. AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS, DTS-HD, TrueHD, FLAC, ALAC and more decoded in the app.
Subtitles
SRT, ASS, WebVTT, PGS and DVB from the file or a sidecar beside it. Size, position, delay, a box behind the text and a preferred language.
Room
A cinema screen you place where you like, or one of ten or more environments with adjustable lighting. Controllers or hand tracking.
Privacy
No account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads. Your servers and passwords stay on the headset. Privacy policy and terms of use.
Price
Free to try. One purchase unlocks it for good.

Free and open source

VR ports and mods of other people's projects, published on GitHub. Each builds on its upstream project and is credited there. None is on a store; they are built or sideloaded.

  1. Moonlight XR

    GitHub ↗

    Moonlight for Android as a native OpenXR app. Streams your PC games to the headset and shows them in 3D, with the depth made on the headset, so nothing changes on the PC. Built for Quest 3 and PICO 4 Ultra.

  2. CitraVR Pico Port

    GitHub ↗

    Amanda Watson's CitraVR, the VR build of the Citra 3DS emulator, ported to PICO 4 and PICO 4 Ultra. Stereoscopic 3DS homebrew and your own backups on a big, movable screen. Beta.

  3. Might and Magic 7 VR mod

    GitHub ↗

    A VR mode for OpenEnroth, the open source reimplementation of the Might and Magic engine, through OpenXR on Windows. Needs your own copy of Might and Magic VII.

  4. Final Fantasy VII Remake VR mod

    GitHub ↗

    An unofficial VR mod for the Steam version of Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade, with a first person mode and performance as the main goal. Runs on any OpenXR headset. Early days, shared as is.
